Projects

Project 1: Prophetic Memory and Strategic Adaptation: Tibetan Responses to Two Waves of Mongol Intervention (13th–17th Centuries)

This project examines how prophetic narratives were used to navigate and rationalize foreign political interventions in Tibet over four centuries.

Project 2: The Bodhisattva King and the Tantric Adept: Trisong Detsen and Padmasambhava

A comparative study of the evolving relationship between King Trisong Detsen and Padmasambhava as depicted across various historical and hagiographical narratives.
